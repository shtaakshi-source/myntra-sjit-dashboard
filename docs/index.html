How send quantities are calculated (FC stock only; we don't control M-Now or FDC stock)
Quantity per FC = (region DRR × cover days) − current stock in that region's FCs. DRR uses every order from customers in the FC's region over the last 30 days, whether it shipped SJIT, PPMP, from M-Now or an FDC, because the FC ends up feeding all of them.
