How send quantities are calculated (only FC stock is ours to send; M-Now and FDCs are refilled by Myntra from the region's FC)
For each SKU and each region (North → Gurgaon, West → Mumbai, South → Bangalore, East → Kolkata), using the last 30 days:
Regional DRR = SJIT orders shipped from the region's FCs + from its M-Now stores + from its FDCs + PPMP orders from customers in the region (demand SJIT should capture), all per day
Units to send = Regional DRR × cover days − stock already in the region's FCs (− M-Now/FDC stock in the region, if ticked below)
